Kaise khelein / How to play Neon Snake

Steer the neon snake, eat orbs to grow and don't bite the walls or yourself.

  • Mobile par swipe karo ya screen ka D-pad dabao; PC par Arrow keys ya W A S D.
  • Pink orb khao = +1 aur snake lamba hota hai; gold orb gayab hone se pehle khao = +5.
  • Easy mein deewar ke paar nikal jaate ho; Medium aur Hard mein deewar ya apni poonch se takraye to game over.
  • Space / P = pause, M = mute.
