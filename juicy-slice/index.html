Kaise khelein / How to play Juicy Slice

Slice flying fruit with a swipe or mouse drag, chain combos and avoid the bombs.

  • Mobile par ungli se swipe karo, PC par mouse drag karke fruit kaato: har fruit +1.
  • Ek swipe mein 3+ fruit kaato to combo bonus milega; golden fruit = +5.
  • Fruit neeche gir gaya to ek life jaati hai; bomb kaata to game over.
  • Easy / Medium / Hard chuno (keys 1/2/3). P ya Space = pause, M = mute.
