Kaise khelein / How to play Cricket Super Over

Time your shots to hit fours and sixes and chase the target in one over.

  • Ball aapke paas pahunche tab tap karo (PC par Space). Zameen par ghoomta ring band ho tabhi shot maaro – perfect timing par SIX!
  • Screen ke left/right side par tap karo ya ← → keys se shot ki disha chuno.
  • Yorker, bouncer, slower ball aur swing aayegi – bouncer par jaldi mat karo, catch ho sakta hai.
  • 6 ball, 2 wicket mein target chase karo. Match jeeto to agla match aur bada target. P = pause, M = mute.
