Kaise khelein / How to play Zone Royale

Battle royale for phone and PC: 20 players drop onto an island, loot crates for better guns, ammo, medkits and armour, and fight to be the last one standing while the safe zone keeps shrinking.

  • Mobile: screen ke left side par ungli ghuma kar chalo, right side par drag karke nishana lagao – drag karte hi gun apne aap fire karti hai. Right side par chhota sa tap = paas wale dushman par quick shot.
  • PC: W A S D / arrow keys se chalo, mouse se nishana, left-click daba ke rakho to fire. R = reload, P = pause, M = mute.
  • 📦 Crates mein ghuso to loot nikalta hai: Pistol → SMG (tez fire) → Shotgun (paas se khatarnak) → Rifle (door tak). Ammo, ✚ medkit (HP 60 se kam ho to khud lag jaata hai) aur 🛡 armour bhi milta hai.
  • Neela storm har phase mein chhota hota jaata hai – safe zone (safed circle) ke andar raho, bahar har second damage lagta hai. Minimap par agla zone dikhta hai.
  • Pedon ke neeche chhupo – bots door se aapko nahi dekh paate. Ghar, container aur pathar goliyon se bachate hain.
  • Aakhri zinda bache to Chicken Biryani Dinner! 🍗 Score = kills × 100 + placement bonus + survival time (+1500 jeetne par).
