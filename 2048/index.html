Kaise khelein / How to play 2048 Pro

Slide the tiles, merge equal numbers and build the 2048 tile.

  • Mobile par board pe swipe karo (upar, neeche, left, right); PC par Arrow keys ya W A S D.
  • Ek jaise number wali tiles takraati hain to jud kar ek ho jaati hain (2+2=4, 4+4=8...).
  • Har move ke baad nayi tile aati hai; board bhar gaya aur move nahi bacha to game over.
  • 2048 tile banao aur jeeto; best score aapke browser mein save rehta hai.
