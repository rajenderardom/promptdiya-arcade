Kaise khelein / How to play Patakha Blast

Launch rockets into the night sky, burst the golden stars and chain the explosions for big combos.

  • Mobile par aasmaan mein jahan tap karoge, wahin rocket jaakar phatega. PC par click karo, ya arrow keys se nishana lagao aur Space dabao.
  • Golden taare = 10, ghoomti chakri = 20 points. Har phata taara khud bhi phatta hai – paas-paas ke taaron ki chain banao: x2, x3, x5 combo!
  • 3 ka combo = 1 rocket wapas, 5 ka combo ya anar pot = Anar (bada dhamaka). Mithai ka dabba = +3 rockets.
  • Udte kandil (lantern) aur patang ko mat jalao – 3 galti = game over. Rockets khatam hone se pehle level ka target poora karo. P = pause, M = mute.
