About Prompt Diya Arcade

Prompt Diya Arcade is a collection of free HTML5 games you can play right in your browser. There is nothing to download or install and no signup — just tap Play and start.

Every game works on mobile and desktop: swipe and tap on your phone or tablet, or use your mouse and keyboard on a PC or laptop. Games are lightweight, load fast and keep your best scores on your device.

About

Prompt Diya Arcade is made by the creator of the Prompt Diya blog. New casual games are added regularly — arcade, puzzle and classic favourites that are quick to learn and fun to replay.

Privacy

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect personal information. Game progress and high scores are stored only in your own browser (local storage).

This site shows advertisements. Third-party ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings.
