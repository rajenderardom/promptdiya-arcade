Kaise khelein / How to play Patang Fight

Fly your kite, cross strings with rival kites and cut them before they cut yours.

  • Mobile par screen par ungli ghumao; PC par mouse ya arrow keys/WASD se patang udao.
  • Apni dor ko dusri patang ki dor ke upar se cross karo – yeh pench hai. Pench ke waqt patang tez ghumate raho, bar hara ho jaayega aur rival patang kat jaayegi: Bo Kata!
  • Agar pench mein ruk gaye to aapki patang kat sakti hai. 3 patang khatam = game over.
  • 🧵 Charkhi pakdo = tez manja (8 second). Har level mein rivals zyada aur tez hote hain. P = pause, M = mute.
