Kaise khelein / How to play Metro Dash

Run through the metro, switch lanes, jump and roll past trains and barriers.

  • Mobile par left/right swipe karke lane badlo; PC par ← → ya A / D.
  • Swipe up ya ↑ / W = jump (neechi barrier); swipe down ya ↓ / S = roll (oonche gate ke neeche).
  • Coins, 🧲 magnet aur 2x boost uthao; speed badhti rehti hai.
  • Train ya barrier se takkar = game over. P = pause, M = mute.
