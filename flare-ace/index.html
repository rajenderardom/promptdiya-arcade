Kaise khelein / How to play Flare Ace

Fly a fighter jet through 12 levels in 4 worlds (Desert, Frostpeak mountains, Iron Ocean fleet, Inferno volcano), dodge homing missiles with flares, beat a boss in every world and unlock Endless mode.

  • Mobile: screen par kahin bhi ungli ghuma kar jet udao. PC: mouse ya WASD / arrow keys. Cannon apne aap fire hota hai.
  • Red MISSILE LOCK warning dikhe to FLARE dabao (PC par Space ya click) – homing missile flares ke peeche chali jaayegi. Flares apne aap recharge hote hain.
  • MISSILE button (PC par Shift / X / right-click) se homing missiles chhodo – SAM site, flak gun, radar aur bunker jaise ground targets ke liye best.
  • Power-ups: 🛡 Shield, Twin guns, homing rockets, ✚ Repair. P = pause, M = mute.
  • 12 levels, 4 worlds: Desert Storm, Frostpeak (snow mountains), Iron Ocean (naval fleet) aur Inferno Ridge (volcano). Har level ke end mein boss – Storm Gunship, Leviathan Carrier aur Magma Fortress jaise naye bosses. Naye dushman dheere-dheere aate hain: drone swarm, AA train, gunboat, SAM frigate, stealth jet, jammer (lock warning chhupa deta hai) aur laser turret.
  • Level map se koi bhi unlocked level chuno. Har level par 1-3 ⭐ (jitna kam damage, utne zyada stars) – progress aapke browser mein save hota hai. Level clear par Next level ya Retry. Level 12 clear karte hi ∞ Endless mode unlock hota hai.
