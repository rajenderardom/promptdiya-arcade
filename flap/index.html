Kaise khelein / How to play Diya Flap

Tap to lift the glowing diya and glide through the gaps between the pillars.

  • Mobile par screen tap karo; PC par click karo ya Space dabao: diya upar uthta hai.
  • Pillars ke beech ke gap se nikalo: har pillar +1.
  • ★ star pakdo to +3 bonus; pillar ya zameen se takraye to game over.
  • P = pause, M = mute.
