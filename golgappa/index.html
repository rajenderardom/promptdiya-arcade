Kaise khelein / How to play Gol Gappa Catch

Catch falling gol gappas in your plate, dodge chillies and build big combos.

  • Mobile par ungli se plate ko left-right kheencho; PC par mouse ya ← → / A D keys.
  • Gol gappa = 10, pani puri = 20, dahi puri = 30 points. Lagataar pakdo to combo x2, x3, x4.
  • Hari mirchi 🌶️ ya soggy puri pakdi, ya gol gappa neeche gira, to ek life jaati hai. 3 life khatam = game over.
  • Imli bottle = slow-motion, masala box = badi plate, dil = extra life. Har 20 gol gappe par speed badhti hai. P = pause, M = mute.
