Kaise khelein / How to play Bubble Pop Shooter

Aim, shoot and match three or more bubbles of the same colour to pop them.

  • Mobile par ungli drag karke nishana lagao aur chhodo; PC par mouse se aim karke click karo.
  • Same rang ke 3 ya zyada bubbles milao to woh phoot jaate hain.
  • Latakte bubbles gira do to bada bonus milta hai; bubbles neeche line tak pahunche to game over.
  • Neeche chhote bubble pe tap karke rang badlo. P = pause, M = mute.
