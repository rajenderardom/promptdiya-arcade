Kaise khelein / How to play Gem Swap

Swap neighbouring gems to line up three or more and reach the level target.

  • Mobile par gem ko swipe karo ya do paas wale gems tap karo; PC par mouse se click ya drag karo.
  • Ek jaise 3+ gems line mein lagao to woh blast hote hain.
  • 4 gems = line blast, 5 gems = color bomb: bade combos banao.
  • Moves khatam hone se pehle target score pahuncho aur agla level kholo.
