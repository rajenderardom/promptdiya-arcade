Kaise khelein / How to play Highway Rush

Switch lanes to dodge traffic, collect coins and use nitro in this endless racer.

  • Mobile par left/right tap ya swipe karke lane badlo; PC par ← → ya A / D.
  • Traffic se bacho aur coins uthao (+25); speed dheere dheere badhti rehti hai.
  • Nitro ke liye swipe up, ⚡ button, ya ↑ / W dabao: gaadiyon ke beech se nikal jaoge.
  • Kisi gaadi se takkar = game over. P = pause, M = mute.
