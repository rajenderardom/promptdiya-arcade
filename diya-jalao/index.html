Kaise khelein / How to play Diya Jalao

Rotate the rangoli path tiles so the flame from the big diya reaches every little diya.

  • Kisi bhi tile par tap/click karo – uska rangoli raasta 90° ghoom jaayega. PC par arrow keys se tile chuno aur Space dabao.
  • Beech wala bada pital (brass) ka diya aag ka source hai. Raaste jod kar flame ko har chhote diye tak pahunchao – jud gaya to diya jal uthega.
  • Har level mein limited moves hain. Kam moves mein solve karo to ★★★ aur zyada score; bache hue moves ka bonus bhi milta hai.
  • Level badhne par board 4x4 se 7x7 tak bada hota hai. Moves khatam aur diye adhure = game over. P = pause, M = mute.
