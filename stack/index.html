Kaise khelein / How to play Stack Tower

Drop each sliding block on the tower and build it as high as you can.

  • Mobile par tap karo; PC par click karo ya Space dabao: sliding block gir jaata hai.
  • Jo hissa neeche wale block se bahar latakta hai woh kat jaata hai, block chhota hota jaata hai.
  • Perfect landing karo to block wapas bada hota hai aur combo banta hai.
  • Block poora miss hua to game over. P = pause, M = mute.
