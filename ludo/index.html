Kaise khelein / How to play Ludo Star

Classic Ludo against the computer: roll the dice and race all four tokens home.

  • Aap Red ho. Mobile par tap karke, PC par click karke dice roll karo aur chamakta token chuno.
  • Token bahar nikaalne ke liye 6 chahiye; 6, capture ya ghar pahunchne par extra roll milta hai.
  • Dushman token par utro to woh wapas ghar jaata hai (★ safe box par nahi).
  • Teen baar 6 aaye to turn chali jaati hai. 2 ya 4 players mode chuno.
