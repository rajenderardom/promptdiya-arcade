Kaise khelein / How to play Block Fit

Drag blocks onto the 8×8 board and clear full rows and columns.

  • Mobile par ungli se, PC par mouse se block ko board par drag karke chhodo; rotate nahi hota.
  • Poori row ya column bhar do to woh clear ho jaati hai aur points milte hain.
  • Ek saath kai lines clear karo to combo bonus milta hai.
  • Jab koi block board par fit na ho to game over. M = mute.
